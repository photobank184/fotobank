Subject to mandatory law, a purchase will normally become non-refundable once:

9. General Refund Standard

PhotoBank may issue a full or partial refund, Account credit, replacement download or other remedy where:

  • the law requires it;
  • the purchased Content was not delivered;
  • the downloaded file is materially defective;
  • the wrong Content was supplied because of a Platform error;
  • the Buyer was charged more than once for the same transaction;
  • an unauthorized transaction is verified;
  • the Content is removed because of a substantiated rights claim;
  • the license description contained a material PhotoBank error;
  • a subscription was renewed after a valid prior cancellation;
  • PhotoBank otherwise determines that a remedy is fair and appropriate.

10. Non-Refundable Situations

Subject to mandatory law, a refund will not normally be issued where:

  • the Content was successfully downloaded and is not defective;
  • you changed your mind after downloading the Content;
  • you no longer need the Content;
  • your client rejected the project;
  • you purchased the wrong file through your own error;
  • you failed to review the Content preview or description;
  • you failed to review the applicable license type;
  • you expected exclusive rights under a non-exclusive License;
  • similar Content became available elsewhere;
  • your software cannot open an otherwise valid supported file;
  • your device, connection or storage system caused the problem;
  • you failed to obtain additional third-party permissions;
  • your intended use is prohibited by the License Agreement;
  • your Account was suspended for a policy violation;
  • you failed to use subscription downloads before they expired;
  • you obtained the Content through fraud or misuse.

11. Accidental Purchases

If you believe that you purchased Content accidentally, you should contact PhotoBank promptly.

PhotoBank may consider a refund or Account credit where:

  • the file has not been downloaded;
  • the license has not been used;
  • the request was made without unreasonable delay;
  • there is no indication of fraud or abuse;
  • the transaction can reasonably be cancelled.

An accidental-purchase refund is not guaranteed.

  • provide reasonable technical assistance.
