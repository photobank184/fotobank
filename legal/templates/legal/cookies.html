2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit a website.

Cookies may allow a website to:

  • recognize a browser or device;
  • maintain a user session;
  • remember preferences;
  • protect accounts and transactions;
  • measure website use and performance;
  • provide personalized functionality;
  • support advertising, where enabled and permitted.

Similar technologies may include local storage, session storage, pixels, tags, scripts, software development kits and device identifiers.

References to “cookies” in this Policy include these similar technologies where appropriate.

3. Who Places Cookies?

Cookies used on PhotoBank may be placed by:

  • PhotoBank, where the cookie is set directly by photobank.online;
  • third-party service providers, where a service used by PhotoBank places or reads a cookie.

Cookies placed directly by PhotoBank are commonly called first-party cookies.

Cookies placed by independent providers are commonly called third-party cookies.

4. Types of Cookies by Duration

4.1 Session Cookies

Session cookies normally remain active only while your browser is open and are deleted when the browser session ends.

They may be used for:

  • maintaining login sessions;
  • protecting forms against security attacks;
  • remembering temporary actions;
  • supporting checkout and download processes.

4.2 Persistent Cookies

Persistent cookies remain on your device for a defined period or until you delete them.

They may be used to:

  • remember language or display preferences;
  • remember cookie choices;
  • recognize returning users;
  • measure Platform performance over time;
  • support permitted analytics and marketing.

5. Categories of Cookies We May Use

5.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies are required for the Platform to operate securely and provide services requested by the user.

They may be used to:

  • authenticate users;
  • maintain secure sessions;
  • protect forms against cross-site request forgery;
  • prevent fraud and abuse;
  • remember privacy choices;
  • support purchases and downloads;
  • balance server traffic;
  • maintain essential Platform functionality.

Because these cookies are necessary to provide the Platform, they cannot normally be disabled through the PhotoBank cookie preference tool.

You may be able to block them through your browser, but parts of PhotoBank may then stop working correctly.

5.2 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow PhotoBank to remember optional settings and provide enhanced features.

They may be used to remember:

  • preferred language;
  • region or currency display;
  • search and filter preferences;
  • layout and accessibility choices;
  • recently viewed Content;
  • other user-selected settings.

Where required by applicable law, functional cookies will be used only after you have provided consent.

5.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors and registered users interact with PhotoBank.

They may collect information about:

  • pages visited;
  • time spent on pages;
  • navigation paths;
  • search activity;
  • downloads and conversions;
  • browser and device characteristics;
  • technical errors;
  • website speed and performance.

We use this information to improve the Platform, identify technical issues and understand which services are useful.

Where consent is required, analytics cookies will not be activated before you accept them.

5.4 Advertising and Targeting Cookies

If PhotoBank introduces advertising or remarketing services, advertising cookies may be used to:

  • measure advertising campaigns;
  • limit how often an advertisement is displayed;
  • show more relevant promotional content;
  • understand whether users interact with advertisements;
  • create or use interest-based audience segments.

Advertising cookies will be used only where enabled and where permitted by applicable law.

Where consent is required, these cookies will remain disabled until you choose to accept them.

5.5 Social Media Cookies

If social sharing, social login or embedded social media content is added to PhotoBank, the relevant provider may place cookies.

These cookies may allow the provider to:

  • recognize signed-in users;
  • measure interaction with embedded content;
  • connect activity across websites;
  • support sharing and login functionality.

The use of these cookies is also governed by the privacy and cookie policies of the relevant provider.

Where required by applicable law, PhotoBank will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device.

  • accept all optional cookies;

You may change or withdraw your cookie choices at any time through the PhotoBank cookie preference tool.

10. Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to:

  • view stored cookies;
  • delete individual or all cookies;
  • block cookies from particular websites;
  • block third-party cookies;
  • disable all cookies;
  • receive warnings before cookies are stored.

Browser settings vary. Refer to the help section of your browser for instructions.

Blocking all cookies may prevent you from:

  • signing into your Account;
  • maintaining a secure session;
  • completing purchases;
  • downloading licensed Content;
  • using other essential Platform functions.

11. Global Privacy Control and Similar Signals

Some browsers and extensions transmit privacy preference signals such as Global Privacy Control.

Where applicable law requires PhotoBank to recognize a valid opt-out preference signal, we will process that signal in accordance with the relevant legal requirements.

Traditional “Do Not Track” signals may not be recognized where no applicable and technically consistent standard exists.

12. Cookie Retention

Cookies are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for their stated purpose.

Retention depends on:

  • the type of cookie;
  • whether it is a session or persistent cookie;
  • security requirements;
  • the user’s chosen preferences;
  • the settings of a third-party provider;
  • applicable legal obligations.

PhotoBank will periodically review cookie durations and remove cookies that are no longer necessary.
